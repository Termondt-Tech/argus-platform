## What changed

### 1. Short strike selected on mid-implied delta (config + `src/`)

→ [decision](../decisions/2026-09-21-short-strike-selection-on-mid-implied-delta.md)

The short was chosen on the **feed** delta (up to 120s stale) while `min_credit`
tests a credit built from **mids**; the floor then selects the strikes the feed
under-states. The five live call entries of 2026-09-21 were ~0.19 delta, not 0.15.
Now `signal.short_delta_source = "mid"` picks the OTM leg whose delta implied by its
own mid (`signal/implied.py`, Black-76 on the parity forward, every convention
imported from `regime.py`) is closest to target.

- **No fallback to the feed delta.** A missing regime/remaining life refuses as
  `no_short_leg_with_mid_delta`.
- The other source's pick rides every record as `alt_pick_strike`; `delta_source`
  splits the dataset. **Never pool call outcomes across this deploy.**
- `target_short_delta` (0.15) and `min_credit` (0.50) are **unchanged**; read the
  decision's Consequences before approving.
- Nothing reads the new keys (AST test).
- **Rollback:** `short_delta_source = "feed"` — a config-only release, after the
  close, never with a position open.
- `tools/replay_selection.py` is the pre-deploy gate tool (read-only).

### 2. Loss-budget sizing (`src/` only)

→ [decision](../decisions/2026-09-21-loss-budget-sizing.md)

`daily_max_loss` becomes a bound on the whole **day**, not only on what is open at
once. Sizer and gate (`daily_loss_budget_exceeded`) both hold `daily_max_loss − W`,
W = today's realized loss + full-width worst case of everything open, the worse of a
ledger estimate and (live only) a broker one. Every unreadable input is ZERO budget.

- **Not a latch; never trips the kill switch** (durable would mean permanent).
- **The bound holds to within the day's fees and the last entry's walk
  concession — accepted, not fixed here.** Sizer and gate price a proposal at the
  mid credit; the entry walk may fill up to 10% lower, so the opened position can
  carry ≤ ~$10/contract (≤ ~$20 at 2 lots) more than was approved. It does not
  compound, since the next gate reads the real fill. Worst case, the day ends around
  −970 against −950. The fix (price the budget half at the walk's floor) is owed
  next release → [decision](../decisions/2026-09-21-loss-budget-sizing.md#as-built),
  open question 1.
- New `risk/loss_budget.py`; `SessionState.day_ledger` is rehydrated from today's
  `order.fill` rows, like the breaker.
- No config key. The welded invariant (`1 × 950 ≤ 950`) is unchanged.
- **Fixed in pre-tag review (2026-09-21): the scan can no longer re-open a closed
  spread.** Right after an exit, the fill ledger already holds the closing debit while
  the watchdog's scan still lists the spread for up to one 1s pass. Because the rule
  takes the larger of the two per trade idea, W counted the realized loss AND the full
  width. The budget still read as KNOWN (2 lots at 0.60, closed at 1.50: −230
  remaining instead of 770). A candidate in that window was refused as
  `daily_loss_budget_exceeded`, which also used up the day's one
  `daily_loss.budget_exhausted` page, so the real exhaustion later would not page.
  Now an idea the ledger has **seen close today** (`Exposure.closed`) is dropped from
  the scan side. An idea the ledger does not know, or holds open, is unchanged. The
  randomized day simulation now also keeps closed spreads on a stale scan, and in
  cert asserts the ledger's W equals the true W at every entry.

## Why it's safe

- Item 2 can only refuse or shrink an entry. Item 1 changes **which** strike is
  sold. On calls that is farther out on average (~0.19 → ~0.15 delta), but on any
  tick it can pick a closer strike where the feed over-states delta. It also makes
  more ticks selectable, so entry frequency can move either way (decision,
  Consequences 2 and 5).
- No exit, watchdog, kill-switch or reconciliation code path changed in behaviour.
- `git diff v0.4.5 --stat -- src argus.*.toml`: 21 files, config diff is the one
  key + comment block in each profile.

## After deploy: what to check

1. `version: v0.4.6`, `config.sha b941e29cc1bf`.
2. First session: `signal.candidate` carries `delta_source: "mid"` and
   `alt_pick_strike`; watch for `no_short_leg_with_mid_delta` volume (a refusal, not
   a fill).
3. Entry short deltas near 0.15, not ~0.19; fewer/lower-credit candidates is the
   expected cost.
4. `forward_source` should read `"parity"` on most rows, with `forward` within a
   point or so of `underlying_price`. The pre-deploy replay could only run the
   `"spot"` fallback (recorded rows hold two legs per side, not the chain), so the
   first session is the first real run of the parity forward.
5. `daily_loss_budget_exceeded` denials on `gate.decision` — first live read of the
   loss budget; confirm the ledger estimate agrees with `order.fill` cash. A denial
   seconds after an exit, with `open_worst_case` > 0 while flat, would mean the
   stale-scan fix is not holding.
6. **After a manual close at the broker** (the C5 SOP), that spread stays counted at
   full width for the rest of the day. A broker-side close is not an Argus order, so
   it never reaches the fill ledger. That is the safe direction, but later entries
   are sized as if it lost the whole width.
