## Fixed

**The greeks feed was held to a price bound, and it is not a price.**
`staleness.market_data_max_age_s` (2.0s) gated both a leg's quote AND its
greeks — right for the quote, which drives pricing and streams continuously by
design, and wrong for the greeks, which are read in exactly one place
(`strategy._select`'s `min()`, choosing the short strike by delta magnitude) and
never touch the credit, the width, the max loss, the sizing, or the walk's
limit. Measured on 2026-08-27: the greeks feed refreshes the whole SPXW chain in
synchronized bursts, not a steady trickle — gaps of median **137s**, p90
**421s**, max **452s**. At a 2.0s bound, a short strike was selectable on **2.8%
of in-window ticks**, with the ladder fully priceable the entire time (0
`signal.skip`); 550 of 565 ticks reported `no_short_leg_with_fresh_delta`.

**This is a regression dating to 2026-08-24, not a longstanding condition** — the
first pass at this release said otherwise and was wrong. The daily series is
unambiguous: candidates ran 2846 / 3115 / 2538 on 08-19 / 08-20 / 08-21 with
`no_short_leg_with_fresh_delta` at **0.0%** and 6 / 8 / 12 fills a day, then
collapsed to **61 candidates at 77%** on 08-25 and **52 at 88%** today. A 40x
drop on one date, and that date is the **v0.3.7 deployment** — the release
carrying A1, the entry-leg freshness gate. Before A1, `_admit` tested `is None`
and nothing else: no age check on greeks existed, ever, until 08-24. The feed
was always this slow; nothing had ever looked. (The market-data source did NOT
change then — `source = "live"` was set 2026-07-20 and never toggled, correcting
an attribution in the 2026-08-24 record.)

**Which makes the fix a restoration, not a relaxation.** For the fifteen sessions
before A1, Argus selected short strikes off deltas of completely unbounded age
and produced ordinary 5-wide 2-contract spreads at 4–12 fills a day, all of them
in `docs/reconciliation.txt`. That is the natural experiment for "does a stale
delta produce bad spreads," and the answer in the record is no. 120s where there
used to be infinity, with telemetry the unbounded era never had.

What a stale delta actually costs is one number: at γ ≈ 0.0075 delta/pt on a
0DTE 15-delta SPX strike, one 5-point strike is 0.0374 of delta, so a 120s-old
reading moves the *selection* by 0.46 of a strike at 1σ and cannot mis-price
anything. **Fixed** with `staleness.greeks_max_age_s` (120.0), a separate
REQUIRED key — `_admit` (`signal/live_chain.py`) now takes two bounds, a leg is
admitted on its quote alone and becomes short-selectable only when its greeks
also clear the new one. Deliberately not sized to the 421s p90 tail, which
would cost 0.86 of a strike at 1σ; the tail is what a DRIFT-based bound
(`γ × |spot moved since capture|`) is for, and this release ships the telemetry
that sizes its tolerance rather than the bound itself —
`ChainCoverage.min_greeks_age_s` (the freshest greeks reading in the chain,
which keeps reporting once the bound stops filtering anything, unlike
`short_admissible`), `OptionQuote.greeks_age_s` traveling with every leg whether
or not its delta cleared, and `signal.none` / `signal.candidate` both carrying
it per side. **Not cert-only** — `[marketdata] source` is already `"live"`, so
this cadence is production behaviour, and `config.py` places no environment
ceiling on it. Config validation refuses a greeks bound tighter than the quote
bound: a leg is admitted on its quote first, so a tighter greeks number would
just be `market_data_max_age_s` wearing a second name.
→ [greeks selector bound and rejection visibility](../decisions/2026-08-27-greeks-selector-bound-and-order-rejection-visibility.md)

**The account-stream staleness bound was calibrated for a healthier cert than
the one Argus was actually talking to.** Of sixteen candidates that survived the
greeks bound, eight were denied at the gate — every single denial
`account_stream_stale`, alone. Cert's `/positions` was timing out often enough
that the REST liveness re-seed could not stamp the account stream at all: 245
`account.refresh_failed` records across 48 outage episodes in one session, worst
outage 92.6s, against a 30.0s bound. The stream itself was healthy throughout
(`connected: true`, `reconnects: 0`); it was the liveness *proof* that kept
expiring, on a book that was flat the whole session. **Fixed**:
`staleness.account_stream_max_age_s` 30 → 120, sized to the day's worst outage
with margin. **This one IS cert-only**, the opposite posture from the greeks
bound above — live's `/positions` has no equivalent latency problem, and this
rail is what stops an entry being sized against an account picture nothing has
proven current. Fenced structurally rather than left to a comment:
`config.py` now refuses `environment == "live"` above 30.0, in the same seam
`audit.synchronous`'s durability refusal already uses.
→ [same doc](../decisions/2026-08-27-greeks-selector-bound-and-order-rejection-visibility.md)

**Every order the broker accepted was rejected — seven of seven — and Argus
recorded no reason for any of them.** `1422817, 1422841, 1423005, 1423158,
1423174, 1423275, 1423293`, spanning 10:30–13:15 ET on 2026-08-27, both puts and
calls, every one `Routed → Rejected` in under a second with zero walk steps
taken. (The release's first draft said "two" — that came off a midday DB
snapshot; the day's full alert trail carries seven, and a 100% refusal rate is a
different finding from two isolated ones.) Five further submissions never landed
at all (`timeout_error`, `empty_response`) and correctly resolved `unresolved`
after confirming no order existed under their identifier. CLAUDE.md's live-fire
pass lists rejections under NOT-YET-EXERCISED; this was the first. The SDK's
`PlacedOrder.reject_reason` field existed the whole
time — `grep reject src/argus/` before this release returns exactly one file,
`execution/fake.py` — and `map_placed_order` mapped `status`,
`replaces_order_id`, `replacing_order_id`, and stopped. Worse, the record that
would have carried the reason, `order.event`, was unconditionally Tier 2 —
queued and droppable, correct for the common case (a status flap on a live
order, re-sent every change) and wrong for the terminal record of a trade idea,
which in the common case has no other record naming the broker's verdict.
**Fixed**: `Order.reject_reason`, mapped through duck-typed with a `getattr`
default so a dry-run echo and a test stub still map safely, bounded at 500
characters before it reaches the canonical hasher, empty string normalized to
`None`. `order.event` is now Tier 1 the FIRST time an order is seen
`disposition == "dead"` — deduped on `broker_order_id`, never
`external_identifier` (a walk reprices; several rungs share one identifier, and
deduping on it would swallow the second rung's own rejection). `FILLED` stays
excluded — its record is `order.fill`. Nothing decides on the string;
`classify_order` still reads `status` alone, because broker prose changes
wording without notice and a rail built to match it would fail open the day it
does.
→ [same doc](../decisions/2026-08-27-greeks-selector-bound-and-order-rejection-visibility.md)

## Deploy

Rebuild AND config move together.

```bash
cd /opt/argus
scripts/argus-deploy.sh --dry-run v0.3.12   # show the plan, touch nothing
scripts/argus-deploy.sh v0.3.12             # deploy it
```

`argus.toml` gains `staleness.greeks_max_age_s` (required, 120.0) and changes
`staleness.account_stream_max_age_s` (30.0 → 120.0); `git checkout v0.3.12`
brings both in the same commit as the code that needs them.

**Rollback needs BOTH halves.** `git checkout v0.3.11` (or whichever prior tag)
reverts the config too — confirm with `git status --short` after checkout,
same discipline v0.3.11's own notes call out.

## Scope

**Closes the residual v0.3.8 (Ain al-Rami) left open, and with it a nine-day
regression**: `short_admissible` sat near zero against a full `admissible` for
97% of a live-quote session, and the cause was the SAME threshold gating both
roles, not a further greeks-feed problem. The 2026-08-24 role split (admit on
quote, select on greeks) was right; keeping one number for both was the
unfinished half — and that half cost 08-24 through 08-27 a ~40x reduction in
candidate flow against the fifteen sessions before it.

**Records a correction to the 2026-08-24 decision record**, appended there as a
dated addendum rather than rewritten: that record attributes the starvation's
appearance to the first live-quote RTH session, and git shows
`[marketdata] source = "live"` unchanged since 2026-07-20. Its measurements
stand; the causal story around them was wrong, and the daily series shows the
step lands on the v0.3.7 (A1) deployment instead.

**Also closes the first-ever `Rejected` order with no visibility gap behind
it** — a condition CLAUDE.md's live-fire pass explicitly flagged as
NOT-YET-EXERCISED and therefore unproven.

**Ships telemetry for a fix it does not make.** `min_greeks_age_s` and
`short.greeks_age_s` land in this release specifically so the DRIFT-based bound
that should eventually replace the 120s timer has a day of real traffic to size
its tolerance from, rather than the three disagreeing estimates (2.7%–4.4%
duty cycle, 137s median gap) that went into picking 120.0 for now.

**One question raised and settled during review, not left implicit:** should
`reject_reason` have shipped alone, ahead of the two staleness bounds, so
nothing raised throughput into an unread rejection? Rejected — in cert, more
rejections cost log lines and nothing else, and the reason lands on the first
one whether it is rejection #1 or #40. All three fixes ship as one deploy.

**Two things explicitly NOT done, each queued behind a stated dependency:**
- **The drift-based greeks bound itself.** Needs `spot_at_greeks_capture`
  threaded through `MarketDataPort` and both adapters, and needs a day of this
  release's own telemetry to size its tolerance from. Building it now would be
  sizing it from the same disagreeing estimates the interim bound already had to
  live with.
- **`NestedOptionChain` in place of `get_option_chain`** for the startup chain
  fetch. A real efficiency win — `Strike` maps 1:1 onto `OptionContract`, plus
  `tick_sizes` and `settlement_type` Argus currently hardcodes or assumes — but
  `Option.active` / `is_closing_only` / `stops_trading_at` exist only in the
  flat form and are the fields most likely to explain a sub-second
  post-routing rejection like the two this release finally has a `reject_reason`
  to read. Waits on that field actually landing on one.

**The daily max-loss circuit breaker remains structurally inert in sandbox**,
unchanged by this release. Proving that rail out is still a live-flip gate.
