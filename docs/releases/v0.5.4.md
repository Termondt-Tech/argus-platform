## Name

**Mirzam**, β Canis Majoris, Arabic *al-mirzam*, **"the announcer" / "the herald"**:
it rises before Sirius and announces it. Arabic astronomers grouped it with **Gomeisa** (β
Canis Minoris, spent on v0.4.1) as *Al Mirzamani*, "the heralds" (of Sirius).
- **Sources, independent:** Ian Ridpath, [Star Tales: Canis
  Major](http://www.ianridpath.com/startales/canismajor.html); [Wikipedia, Beta Canis
  Majoris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beta_Canis_Majoris); [David Darling,
  Mirzam](https://www.daviddarling.info/encyclopedia/M/Mirzam.html). Allen's gloss is "the
  announcer"; Danielle Adams gives "bringer-forth", so the sources vary at the margin.
- **How it qualifies:** by tradition, not literal "eye" etymology — the standing already given
  to **Vindemiatrix** (v0.5.0, "the herald of the vintage") and Alterf. **Found outside the
  pool, which is empty**; it spends no reservation.
- **The fit is the release's shape.** Half of it makes the record announce what the watchdog
  decided on the tick it acted (P11), and the other half lets a distant position be watched
  to the close instead of flattened. A herald is the one that reports what the eye saw
  before the event, not the event. It is also the **other half of Gomeisa's pair**, the
  third time a release has taken a spent star's companion (Zubenelgenubi, Gorgonea Tertia).

**Alternative: Errai / Alrai** (γ Cephei), Arabic *ar-rāʿī*, "the shepherd" — the star that
succeeds Polaris as the pole star (~3000–4000 CE). A shepherd watches a flock and does not
chase every wolf, which fits "watch, don't flatten, when it is far". It fits the deferral
half only, and not the record half. [Wikipedia, Gamma
Cephei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamma_Cephei); [EarthSky, Gamma Cephei, a future North
Star](https://earthsky.org/brightest-stars/gamma-cephei-errai-future-north-star/).

**Chosen by Richard 2026-09-28.** Errai was the alternative and was not taken.

### 1. The flatten deferral's k: 10 → 2 (config only)

| Key | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| `flatten_defer_insurance_multiple` (k, policy) | 10.0 | **2.0** |
| `flatten_defer_tail_z99` (calibration) | 2.33 | **2.38** |

| `flatten_defer_source` | the 2026-09-25 provenance | the 2026-09-28 provenance (required, refused if blank) |

**What the operator sees.** The 15:30 flatten is skipped only when its one-tick cost ($5 a
lot) is at least **twice** the expected loss it insures, that loss valued at the spread's
full width, with the touch probability from the calibrated tail. On a 5-wide that is
**z ≥ 3.05**, up from a boundary of 5.12. On the 41 package-B survivors in 24 sessions the
replay skips 23 (7 at k = 10). Positions the deferral now leaves alone are held to
expiry with the strike-touch stop live to the close.
- **Frequency of the risk:** modelled at about one skipped position every two years reaching
  its strike before the close (upper bound). A touch is exited by the touch stop at about
  $250 a lot; if that exit also fails, at most width minus credit, about $445.
- **Across both slots:** at most $890, inside `daily_max_loss` 1050. The loader still
  refuses `on` without the welded invariant.
- **Gain vs k = 10:** +$1.1 to +$3.9 per day. Small. The decision's reason to move is that
  the model is data-checked at k = 2 and extrapolated at k = 10, and the position that
  prompted it was a plain case for skipping.
- **It changes nothing else about the deferral**: the flatten-only removal, the exclusions,
  per-tick re-decision, the "restart after 15:00 disables it" rule.

### 3. P11: the deferral decision is on `watchdog.triggered` (image)

`_deferral_field(deferral)` in `watchdog.py` is now the ONE builder of the `flatten_deferral`
field for `watchdog.check`, `watchdog.triggered` and `watchdog.triggered_still`.
`PositionWatchdog._trigger` takes `deferral` as a **required keyword**.
- **Before.** A flatten the deferral declined to defer, which is most of them, wrote no z,
  P_touch or lhs anywhere. k could not be priced on live flattens.
- **Now.** That tick's decision is on the triggered record. **Absent, never null, on a tick
  the deferral did not decide on** (mode `off`, no flatten in `fired`, a spent position), so
  every older row keeps its exact shape.
- **Records only.** Nothing in `_trigger` decides on it.

## Rules to carry

- **k is policy; the z99 / alpha pair is a calibration output, never hand-tuned.** Lowering k
  needs the rerun and a decision record (§4.9); raising it is the safe direction.
- **One builder for the deferral field (`_deferral_field`).** Never spell it inline again.
- **`_trigger`'s `deferral` is required, with no default.** A caller that forgot it would
  silently drop the decision from the record.
- **A deferral only ever REMOVES the flatten.** It never constructs a price, and it is
  never allowed for a spent, failed-send, blind or unreachable position.
- **The welded invariant** `max_concurrent_positions × max_loss_per_position ≤ daily_max_loss`
  still gates `flatten_deferral = "on"`.
- **Regenerate the blackout file with `--omit CPI --omit NFP --omit PCE`**, the flags its
  header names. FOMC can never be omitted, and the shipped-file test fails if the flags are
  dropped.

## Why it's safe

- **The config change moves a boundary in the direction that holds more, on a position that
  is far from its strike, with the strike-touch stop live to the close.** The worst case is
  bounded and inside the day's cap (above).
- **The tail model is checked where k now sits**: at or above the observed frequency at
  every boundary, zero violations in the rerun's backtest, and the largest standardized
  15:30–16:00 move in 26 sessions is 1.93 against a 3.05 boundary.
- **The recalibration moved the tail heavier**, the conservative direction, so it is adopted
  with k. On the old values k = 2's boundary would be 2.95.
- **The image change is additive**: a field on records, no new send path, no decision reads
  it. Tests: `tests/test_watchdog_flatten_deferral.py` (+99 lines) and
  `tests/test_flatten_deferral.py` (+28).
- **The release days are traded under every rail that bounds any other day**: the touch
  stop, one per side, 2 × 475, and the 1050 loss budget. A post-release trend day costs the
  book's usual bad day, not a new kind of loss. The −$600 abort caps the experiment.
- **Evidence limits, stated:** 24–29 sessions of one regime, model marks for the flatten's
  price, and no `flatten_deferral_outcome` row on record yet (no deferral has run live).
  The decision calls the evidence exploratory.

## What this release does NOT do

- **It does not change the strike-touch stop, the caps, the side rule, the profit target
  (still off), the loss budget or any size rail.**
- **It does not add a config key** or a migration, and it changes no runtime blackout code.
- **It does not change FOMC or the discretionary dates.**
- **It does not license lowering k further.** k = 1 and k = 3 were weighed and rejected in
  the decision; anything below 2 needs its own rerun and record.
- **It does not make the deferral evidence-complete.** The first live outcomes (below) are
  still owed.
- **It acts on none of the objective audit's findings beyond P3, P4 (k), P11, P12 and P13.**
  The rest are proposals, measurements or holds; see the due diligence.
- **It does not change what v0.5.2's other first-occurrence reads owe** (the strike-touch
  stop's review triggers, the first two-slot day, the first budget page).
